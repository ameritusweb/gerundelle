# The SOV fiction corpus

Generated by `python -X utf8 tools/corpus.py`: every scene of the ladder (rungs 0-6, 6 with the bolt sorting
last, 8, 9) x six temperaments (temperament.py: steady, impatient, restless, dogged, trusting, skeptical) x both
minds (the lean SOV layer; Gerundelle). No language model anywhere: minds act in Gerundelle's force world, their
ledgers become gerund ASTs, a construction printer writes the prose, and the determination system reads every
sentence back.

- `stories.jsonl`: one story per line. `sentences[i].label` is the reader's skeleton for the sentence, kept only
  when it has the shape the construction intended (`shape`); `plan` and `stage` say what the sentence tells
  (attempt, explore, learn, feel, ask, tell, ...) and where in the arc (setup, tension, turn, resolution).
  `duplicate_of` marks a story identical to an earlier one (same sentences), kept for the record.
- `summary.md`: the table of runs.

Run 1: 120 runs, 66 unique stories (Gerundelle 40, lean 26), all of them with every sentence read back: 2212
labelled sentences, 474 distinct; 20 root gerunds, 86 distinct labels. Outcomes: 54 reached, 8 gave up, 4
unresolved (the run ended first). Limits: three worlds' worth of objects and one lexicon per world, so sentences
repeat across stories; trusting and skeptical differ only where someone tells something (rungs 2, 3, 6).

## Run 3 (2026-10-26): 25 scenes

Scenes: the ladder's (rungs 0-6, 6 fair, 8, 9), eight more of Gerundelle's curriculum worlds (rungs 10-17: a boulder
on a plank, a toy cannon, a catapult, a doorbell with its wire out, a chime with a damper, a chain on a shelf, a
chest and a tin), and seven dressings of them (rungs 18-24: a music box, a garden gate, a diary, a log in a stream,
a pinball launcher, a radio, a bicycle bell). Every lexicon phrase has alternatives (variants.py), taken in turn
from each story's seed; a story longer than 90 sentences is kept but marked `too_long`.

300 runs, 300 stories; **259 clean** (unique, every sentence read back, under the cap): **6634 labelled
sentences, 1447 distinct**; 101 distinct labels over 21 root gerunds. **187 differ in what happens** (the sequence
of plan kinds and root gerunds), the rest in wording. Lean 126, Gerundelle 133. Outcomes: 232 reached, 15 gave up,
12 unresolved; 4 too long.

## Run 4 (2026-10-26): people, lies and loss in the new worlds

62 scenes: run 3's 25 and 37 generated social scenes (social.py: for nine worlds with a fastening, Ben told /
Leo lies / Leo keeps it shut; loss for the four with a prize; six two-scene pairs with Ben). 744 runs, 744 stories:
**662 clean, 502 distinct plots, 20531 labelled sentences (2800 distinct), 110 labels over 21 root gerunds.**
Social: 403 clean (lie 105, keep 98, told 93, pair 64, loss 43), 315 distinct plots. Outcomes: 590 reached, 58 gave
up, 14 unresolved; 11 too long. Scene names: an integer is a ladder rung; "world:kind" or "a>b" a social scene.
