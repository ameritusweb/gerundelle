# cognitivechess_v2: standard chess, matching warpchess rung 4p

Started 2026-11-16.

## Decided (the user, 2026-11-16)

- A cognitivechess_v2 that matches what was done in warpchess: rung 4p's stack (the plans territory, the tool, commit, in-game dreams, the mind in the opponent's shoes, the runner with a checkpoint after every game), on standard chess.
- **Standard chess, with checkmate read as the king's piece gain.** Thwarting the opponent's plans includes the plan to checkmate.

  How that reads on the chess board (my statement of it, after building; my proposal, which the user agreed to, said "a mate in one is a one-move plan", and that was imprecise): a **one-move plan on my king** is being in check now (their turned board can take it); it is thwartable when I have a legal escape. A **two-move plan on my king** is a mating move of theirs: after it I have no reply, so "whatever I reply" holds with nothing to reply, and the king's capture follows. Two chess-only rules in plans.py (`no_reply`): after a game-ending first move of theirs, mate leaves only my king as their gain, and stalemate leaves nothing (a draw).
- **The domain layer is copied** from warpchess into this folder and adapted. It is not shared, so a later warpchess edit can't break chess. The Gerundelle mind side is untouched.
- **The opponent is random Black,** as in rung 4p.
- **A chess Rust board,** as warpchess has `warp_board`: the same Python interface, so `rustboard.py` here mirrors warpchess's.

## My readings (for the user to change)

- **Turned boards allow taking the king.** The plans territory reads the opponent's moves on a turned board (the turn handed to them). In chess that board can be illegal: if they have just given check, restoring their turn lets them take the king. The chess board's moves are "every move that doesn't leave the mover's own king attacked", which in any legal position are exactly standard chess's legal moves, and on a turned board where the opponent's king is attacked they include taking it. So "your king taken" is a one-move plan of theirs, thwartable only by a move that escapes it, and an unthwartable one is checkmate. plans.py needs no new plan kind.
- **Pressure** (`press_map`, as warp_board's): a colour's legal moves with the turn set to it and no en passant square, by where each lands; a pawn's push and a castling king's two-square move press nothing.
- **Game over:** checkmate, stalemate, a king missing (a line in a plan search stops there), the fifty-move rule (the game world's clock), and the half-move cap. Insufficient material and repetition are not read (the board keeps no history across pickles, as warp_board's doesn't).
- **The move generation is mine**, with shakmaty for attack tables only: chess crates refuse turned positions as invalid. It is checked field by field against python-chess on random positions (`check_chessboard.py`).
