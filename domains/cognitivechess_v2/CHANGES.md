# Changes from warpchess (copied 2026-11-16)

Every file here except the ones listed as new is a copy of warpchess's (rung 4p's stack, as of 2026-11-14). The Gerundelle mind side (`E:/vscode/analogy/molecules`) is shared and untouched. What differs:

## New

- `chess_board/`: the chess Rust board (pyo3 0.22; its own attack rays and move generation). Moves are every move that doesn't leave the mover's own king attacked: chess's legal moves in a legal position, plus the capture of a king left in check on a turned board.
- `rustboard.py`: warpchess's interface over it, for one 8x8 board: squares 0..63, names like `e4`, moves as UCI. Warp's names stay (`WarpMove`, `WarpBoard`, aliased `ChessMove`, `ChessBoard`); `square_side` is 0, `flip_square` is the identity, no move flips, no crabs.
- `check_chessboard.py`: the board against python-chess (perft, 200 random games, pressure, a turned board).
- `check_plans.py`: warpchess's plan check on chess positions, with three named king positions; differences that are warpchess's documented reading are counted apart from bugs.
- `trace_plan_diff.py`, `trace_plan_diff2.py`: the traces behind that classification.

## Changed

- `warpworld.py`: the header; Warp's room FENs (two boards) replaced by the chess starting position under the same names (rooms not ported); file readings on one board, named `file_a` .. `file_h` (warpchess: `file_La` .. `file_Rh`). Piece identities are named by their starting square (`black_knight_g8`).
- `plansworld.py`: `SQUARES` is all 64 squares (warpchess: the 112 real squares of two 7-rank boards); its header.
- `plans.py`: `no_reply`, used by `two_move` and `still_plan`. With no legal reply of the mind's, the game is over: mate leaves only the mind's king as the opponent's gain (the plan to checkmate), stalemate leaves nothing. In warpchess an empty reply list meant a king already taken.
- `check_plans.py`'s `N` reads the command line only when run as a script.
