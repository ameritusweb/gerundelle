> Where does Berlyne's curiosity and arousal theory fit into the system?

● Here's where it lands in the running system.

  Berlyne (1960, 1966): curiosity is driven by "collative variables" — novelty, complexity, incongruity, and surprise. He distinguished diversive curiosity (seeking stimulation
  and novelty for its own sake) from specific curiosity (seeking information to resolve a specific uncertainty). The Wundt curve (inverted U): moderate novelty is most
  arousing; too little novelty is boring; too much is overwhelming.

  Both kinds of curiosity are in the running system, as separate mechanisms:

  Specific curiosity is the open-slot mechanism. An unfilled slot in the understanding register adds constant, non-decaying tension to its molecule's bid every tick (Params.T
  in molecules.py). The concept map: "An open slot with unfilled binding sites adds a constant, non-decaying tension to the molecule's bid every tick until the site is filled.
  The incomplete thought keeps returning to consciousness not because it's surprising but because it's unfinished. That's the earworm mechanism." This is specific curiosity:
  the mind seeks particular information (what causes this? what conditions this?) to resolve a particular uncertainty. In the fiction ladder, curiosity is a phase that forms
  when a question is posed (Phases.graph: "curiosity: a question posed"), anchored on the concept asked about. "Mara wondered what the latch did." — specific curiosity about
  the latch's role.

  Diversive curiosity is the novelty term in the force between molecules (Mind.force, line ~345). When two molecules have never been paired, the force between them is pure
  novelty: Params.n (0.3 by default). As they accumulate experience together, the novelty term shrinks: n × info_gain(a) / IG0, where info_gain is the expected information
  gain from one more observation of the pair's Dirichlet. A fully explored pair has concentrated Dirichlet counts, low info_gain, and low novelty. The pull of the untried is
  the pull of novelty itself — not directed at a specific question, just at what has not been experienced.

  Params.curiosity is a temperament parameter: "the pull of unknowns no want pays for (an unclaimed region, a held region's uniqueness), as a multiple of the territory's own.
  The curious wander; the incurious stay on the want." The fiction ladder measured this: an impatient Gerundelle Mara (rung 0) tries the flag and the lid but never the latch
  she was curious about, and gives up; a restless Mara (rung 2) never asks Ben, opens the box, learns nothing ("learns many things shallowly"). Curiosity as a parameter gives
  different, nameable characters from the same algebra.

  Berlyne's collative variables map as follows:

  - Novelty: the novelty term in Mind.force (Params.n × info_gain / IG0). Maximum for a never-formed pair. Shrinks with experience. Doubled when the meta layer detects
    "nothing's happening" (boost_until, line ~279: "novelty doubled until this tick").
  - Surprise: arrival surprise (S[j] = -log(base_rate) / log(vocab)) and outcome surprise (prediction error |outcome - mu| / 2). Both raise a molecule's bid and extend its
    half-life (a_surprise modulator).
  - Complexity: bonding depth. The concept map: "Complexity is bonding depth, not an atomic bottom layer. A half-formed thought is a molecule with open binding sites. A
    complex thought is fragments bonded to fragments bonded to fragments." A composite molecule's structural novelty (Mind.novelty: 1 minus the highest Jaccard similarity of
    its signature to any other molecule's) is its uniqueness, and extends its half-life (a_novelty modulator).
  - Incongruity: the conflation detector. When a molecule's outcomes are bimodal (good and bad both above 25%), Meta emits "torn" — two strong pulls in opposite directions.
    The conflict between bindings is incongruity, and it generates a meta molecule that competes for the meta track's slots.

  The Wundt curve (inverted U). The system has a boredom readout (Mind.readouts, line ~706): the mean surprise of the winners. When boredom stays below 0.05 for five ticks,
  the meta layer emits "bored" — a meta molecule that notices "nothing's happening" and doubles the novelty term (boost_until). Too little novelty triggers a boost. The other
  end: a molecule whose surprise is very high (≥ 0.8) emits "surprised" in the meta layer. An overwhelmed mind — too many surprising things at once — would have every slot
  occupied by high-surprise molecules, crowding out the structured thoughts that have open sites and can be built on. The tournament's scarcity naturally limits how many
  surprising things can be attended to at once: the slots cap the novelty. The inverted U is not a parameter — it emerges from the competition between novelty-driven bids
  and structured, tension-driven bids under scarce slots.

  How it differs from Berlyne specifically:

  - Berlyne's collative variables are described qualitatively (novelty, complexity, surprise, incongruity). Here, each is a computed quantity with a specific formula and a
    measurable effect on the bid, the half-life, or the meta layer's output.
  - Berlyne's arousal model is drive-based: curiosity is a drive that seeks reduction through information. Here, curiosity is a competition: open slots pull, novelty pulls,
    surprise pulls, and the tournament allocates attention to whatever pulls hardest under scarcity. There is no single "drive" to reduce — there are many pulls, and the mind
    attends to the winning one.
  - Berlyne doesn't distinguish provenance. Here, specific curiosity (open slots) is funded by evidence with provenance weights. A slot filled by told knowledge (half weight)
    still pulls, less than an empty slot, more than a lived one. The curiosity's intensity tracks the provenance of what has and hasn't been learned.
  - The novelty_share readout (Mind.readouts) measures how much of the winners' bindings come from novelty rather than from experience or fit. A high novelty share means the
    mind is exploring; a low one means it's exploiting what it knows. The share is a diagnostic, not a parameter — it tells you what the mind is doing, not what it should do.
