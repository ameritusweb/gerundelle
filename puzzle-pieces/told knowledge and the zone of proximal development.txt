> Where does Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development fit into the system?

● Here's where it lands in the running system.

  Vygotsky's ZPD (1978): what a learner can do alone, what they can do with help, and what they cannot do even with help. The zone of proximal development is the middle band
  — what the learner can accomplish with guidance from someone more knowledgeable. Learning happens when the teacher scaffolds the learner through tasks in this zone, then
  gradually withdraws.

  Told knowledge at half weight is the mechanism. In the understanding register (molecules.py, Understanding.funding), every receipt carries provenance: lived (1.0), worked
  out (1.0), told (0.5 × Params.credulity), imagined (0.0). What someone tells you enters at half weight. You don't own it until you test it. The Lean proof
  (replay_mass_le) guarantees that no operation can raise the weight — only a Fit event (an interaction with the world) mints lived receipts.

  The chess ladder's rung 2 measured this directly. A mind coached by Stockfish at depth 8:

  - The coach's word enters as told knowledge, funded at half, believed by the planner as far as the mind's credulity goes.
  - When the world contradicts the coach's word, the told way is refuted, the coach's reliability record is marked down, and anger forms at the teller.
  - The mind's own lived records come first: a move the mind has tried and seen do nothing is read off its own record, and the coach's word for it no longer counts.

  The measured result was that coaching hurt: Stockfish made the mind worse (671 defended wins vs 773 alone) because the coach determined which experiences came first, and the
  easy wins it handed over prevented the failures the mind needed to learn the conditions. But — and this is the ZPD point — coaching helped when it saved search the mind
  could not do alone. Rung 2b's book (a mate in two whose first move is quiet) showed the right case: 112 of 120 positions mated with the book vs 37 alone. A told line whose
  steps chain into a plan the mind could not have found by itself is the zone of proximal development: what the learner can do with help.

  The fiction ladder measured the withdrawal. Rung 6: Mara watches her grandfather Ben open the box. His pushes enter as seen receipts (learned by watching: the other's push
  contrasted with her own failure teaches the condition). In the second scene, alone with a cupboard whose bolt plays the latch's role, she recalls the episode by shape
  (Case 2, structure transfers across domains) and holds the condition as recalled — funded at half, a hypothesis to test. Her own tries confirm it: the told receipt becomes
  lived.

  The chess ladder's rung 1b measured what happens when the coach is withdrawn: every Stockfish-trained mind lost the exchange exactly once after imagination was turned off,
  learned the condition from that one lived loss, and went on winning. "The gap between alone and courted": courted plays better sooner, alone ends with more of its own.

  The temperament system gives credulity as a parameter (Params.credulity): how much a mind trusts what it's told. A credulous mind acts on told knowledge at once. A
  skeptical mind tests the alternatives first. On fiction rung 2, skeptical Mara tests the alternatives to Ben's word before acting on it; dogged Mara asks, tests, and is
  right in 26 sentences. Different minds in the same zone, scaffolded differently by the same teacher.

  How it differs from Vygotsky specifically:

  - Vygotsky's ZPD is a zone: tasks inside it are learnable with help, tasks outside it are not. Here, the boundary is structural: told knowledge helps when it saves search
    the mind can't do alone (rung 2b: a quiet first move the mind would never try), and hurts when it shortcuts a failure the mind needed (rung 2: the contrast that teaches
    the condition). The zone is defined by what the mind's own search can reach, not by task difficulty.
  - Vygotsky says nothing about what happens to the scaffolding after it's withdrawn. Here, the withdrawal is measured: one lived failure to recover, then everything the coach
    answered has to be re-asked. The cost of withdrawal is proportional to how much the coach answered that the mind never tested.
  - The provenance is conserved: told stays told. Vygotsky's model has the learner eventually "owning" what the teacher provided. Here, ownership requires a Fit event — the
    learner must test it. The Lean proof says this cannot be bypassed.
