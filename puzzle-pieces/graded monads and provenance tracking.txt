> Where do graded monads fit into the system?

● Here's where it lands in the running system.

  Graded monads (Katsumata, 2014; Orchard, Wadler & others): a refinement of monads in programming language theory. A monad tracks "what kind of computation was done"
  (I/O, state, exceptions). A graded monad tracks it with a grade — a label from an ordered set that says how much of what kind. If your grade is "pure" you did nothing
  effectful. If your grade is "IO" you touched the world. The type system enforces that compositions respect the grading: you can't claim a computation is pure if one of
  its steps was IO. The grading is structural — a type error, not a runtime check.

  Provenance in Gerundelle is a graded writer monad. Each operation on the understanding web carries a grade that says what kind of evidence it produced:

  - Fit (an interaction with the world): grade "lived." Full weight.
  - Attest (someone telling you something): grade "told." Half weight (Params.credulity).
  - Posit (imagining something): grade "imagined." Zero weight.

  The Lean file Monad.lean (25 theorems) formalizes this. The key result: lamarckian_ill_graded — the arrow pairing Pose's geometry move with a lived accrual does not have
  Pose's grade. It is ill-typed in the graded monad. The paper (line ~1514): "Knowledge passed between minds arrives as told, never as lived."

  What this means concretely: Pose shares structure (the geometry of what was understood). It does not share provenance (who experienced what). The grading says Pose's grade
  is "pure with respect to lived evidence" — it moves geometry without minting lived receipts. An arrow that writes a lived event cannot carry Pose's grade, because Pose
  doesn't interact with the world. The Lamarckian pairing — "I lived through it, so my child lives through it too" — is a type error. The child receives the content (what
  the parent learned) but not the experiential warrant. The child's receipt is told, at half weight.

  The concept map: "The Lean proof (replay_mass_le): no operator, no sequence of operators, no interleaving can increase lived mass without a Fit event — an interaction with
  the world. Told receipts fund at a fixed weight set by the Attest discount, and took_told_le proves the weight can never exceed c₀ per trial."

  The grading enforces three structural properties:

  1. The firewall (firewall): imagination cannot reach the funded web. The imagination register has no provenance record (it's a copy of the geometry with no ledger). Anything
     done there has grade "imagined" — zero weight. The inbound morphism (toImag_apply) exists: you can always project funded understanding into imagination. The outbound
     morphism does not exist (no_outbound): you can never reconstruct which web you came from.

  2. Told stays told (took_told_le): told receipts fund at a fixed discount per trial. No composition of operators can increase the told weight past c₀. You can't listen
     harder to make what someone said more true.

  3. Only Fit mints lived receipts (replay_mass_le): the only operator with grade "lived" is Fit — an interaction with the world. Every other operator's grade is lower.
     No sequence of non-Fit operations can increase lived mass. The total lived mass in the system is bounded by the number of Fit events.

  The paper (line ~1526): "Pose's grade is empty because of what Pose is: it shares structure, not experience. With that grade, no arrow that writes a lived event can carry
  it, so the Lamarckian pairing (inheriting the experiential warrant of acquired characteristics) is a type error in the graded monad, not a rule that could be relaxed
  without changing what Pose is."

  How it differs from graded monads in PL theory specifically:

  - In programming language theory, graded monads track effects (IO, state, nondeterminism) and the grading enforces effect bounds at compile time. Here, the "effects" are
    epistemic: the grading tracks what kind of evidence an operation produces, and the bounds enforce that provenance cannot be fabricated.
  - In PL, the grading is about what a program CAN do (IO or not). Here, it's about what a mind DID: the ledger records every operation's grade, and the funding computation
    (Understanding.funding) reads the grades to weight the evidence. The grading is both a type constraint (the Lean proofs) and a runtime quantity (the Python implementation
    reads the provenance weights).
  - The prior-art report found no cognitive architecture that uses graded monads or semiring provenance as its backbone. The connection — that provenance tracking in a mind IS
    a graded writer monad, and that the non-launderability of evidence IS the grading's type constraint — is the paper's own bridge.
