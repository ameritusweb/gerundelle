> Where does metacognition (Flavell, Nelson, etc.) fit into the system?

● Here's where it lands in the running system.

  Metacognition (Flavell, 1979; Nelson & Narens, 1990): thinking about thinking. Monitoring: knowing what you know, noticing when you're confused, detecting when a strategy
  isn't working. Control: deciding to switch strategies, allocating more effort to a hard problem, pausing to reflect. Nelson & Narens' model: a meta-level that monitors an
  object-level and controls it through actions like "terminate," "allocate," and "change strategy."

  The Meta class (molecules.py, line ~1150) is a direct implementation. Meta molecules are thoughts about thinking. They compete in their own track (Params.meta_slots:
  3 → 2 → 2), separate from regular thoughts, so they neither crowd out nor are crowded by the thoughts they're about.

  Each kind of meta molecule is formed when a specific cognitive state is detected:

  | kind       | formed when                                  | predicts                           | operation           |
  | stuck      | subject blocked 3 ticks running               | still open at the deadline         | park it             |
  | looping    | blocked and at the top 3 of the last 5        | at the top again                   | park it             |
  | wrong      | a box of the subject was contradicted          | not re-closed on the same keys     | -                   |
  | torn       | its top binding nearly tied (within 5%)        | its top binding flips              | -                   |
  | surprised  | surprise >= 0.8 while it holds a slot          | another event involving it          | -                   |
  | resolved   | it closed                                     | not re-opened                      | -                   |
  | bored      | mean surprise of winners < 0.05, 5 ticks       | no events                          | novelty doubled     |
  | conflated  | its outcomes bimodal (good and bad both > 25%) | a boundary splits it               | differentiate it    |

  Monitoring is the detection: the meta layer observes the tournament's output (Meta.observe, called every tick in Mind.think) and detects stuck thoughts, contradicted
  beliefs, near-ties, high surprise, closures, boredom, and bimodal outcomes. Each detection creates a meta molecule that enters the meta track's pool.

  Control is the operation: meta molecules that have an operation (Meta.OPERATES: stuck, looping, bored, conflated, distinction, same) compete for the meta track's slots.
  When an operable meta molecule wins the top of its track, its operation fires (Meta.operate):
  - stuck/looping → park the subject (remove from competition, key it so it returns when circumstances change)
  - bored → double novelty for 5 ticks
  - conflated → split the concept on a boundary condition (differentiate)

  Each meta molecule makes a prediction about what happens next (its "predicts" column). The prediction is scored: if confirmed within the meta horizon (Params.meta_horizon =
  5 ticks), the meta molecule's activation is refreshed. If refuted, it fades. This is the meta-level earning its keep: a meta observation that correctly predicts what happens
  next persists. One that doesn't, doesn't.

  Meta molecules bind only to their subjects (Meta.about: meta id → subject id). A "stuck" molecule about the latch binds to the latch, not to the flag. At the top of the
  meta track, a meta molecule about a regular thought is a thought-about-that-thought winning a slot — metacognition as a competitive process, not a separate homunculus.

  The meta layer also gates operations: inquiry (testing a hypothesis) fires only when its meta molecule wins a slot (the user, 2026-10-24: "capability without receptor is
  latent"). An operation that exists but whose meta molecule never wins the competition is latent — the mind has the capability but not the metacognitive awareness to use it.

  The concept map's what_ifs.txt (the thoughts-as-molecules idea): "Some of the thoughts are meta-thoughts exhibiting meta-cognition." This is exactly what the Meta class
  implements: meta-thoughts that are molecules in their own right, competing in their own tournament, binding to their subjects, earning their keep by prediction.

  How it differs from metacognition theory specifically:

  - Nelson & Narens' model has two levels (object-level and meta-level) with monitoring flowing up and control flowing down. Here, meta molecules compete in the same
    architecture as regular molecules, just in their own track. There's no separate "meta-level" — meta molecules ARE molecules, with the same bid computation, the same
    half-life, the same decay. The distinction is in what they're about (a cognitive state, not a percept) and in their separate slots.
  - Params.meta_depth = 2 allows meta-about-meta: a molecule that detects a meta molecule's state (e.g., "the stuck detection itself is stuck"). The recursion is bounded by
    funding — a meta-meta molecule has to earn its keep by correct predictions, same as any meta molecule.
  - Flavell describes metacognitive knowledge (what you know about your own cognition) and metacognitive experience (the feeling of knowing, the feeling of confusion). Here,
    the knowledge is in the meta molecule's kind and prediction ("I notice this thought is stuck, and I predict it will stay open"). The experience is the meta molecule
    winning the top — being conscious of one's own confusion is the "stuck" molecule beating all other meta molecules for its slot.
  - The ablation (Params.mute_meta): meta molecules enter the pool but never bid. They're present but silent. The fiction ladder and chess ladder measured the cost of turning
    off meta: without it, the conflation detector never fires (bimodal outcomes are never split), bored states are never detected (novelty never boosted), and stuck thoughts
    are never parked. Metacognition is load-bearing.
