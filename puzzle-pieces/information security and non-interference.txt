> Where does information security / non-interference fit into the system?

● Here's where it lands in the running system.

  Non-interference (Goguen & Meseguer, 1982): a security property that says information at one level cannot flow to another level. If a system satisfies non-interference,
  then actions at the "high" (secret) level cannot affect observations at the "low" (public) level. No sequence of operations, no interleaving, no channel can move information
  from high to low. It's a structural guarantee about information flow, proved over all possible executions, not checked by testing.

  Provenance in Gerundelle is a non-interference property. Knowledge has three levels: lived (interactions with the world, full weight), told (what someone said, half weight),
  and imagined (what the mind thought up, zero weight). The guarantees, proved in Lean:

  1. The firewall (firewall): anything done in the imagination register is invisible to the funded web. Imagination has geometry without funding, and funding is what makes
     understanding real. You can project a funded web into imagination (the inbound morphism, toImag_apply), but you can never go back (no_outbound: every map from imagination
     back to funded webs gets lived mass wrong on some web). FORMAL_GUARANTEES.md: "There is no function — not just no permitted function, no function at all — that
     reconstructs funding from imagination. The asymmetry is structural."

  2. Told can never become lived (replay_mass_le): no operator, no sequence of operators, no interleaving can increase lived mass without a Fit event — an interaction with the
     world. Told receipts fund at a fixed weight set by the Attest discount, and took_told_le proves the weight can never exceed c₀ per trial.

  3. The Lamarckian pairing — inheriting lived knowledge across generations — is a type error (lamarckian_ill_graded): in the graded writer monad that tracks provenance, the
     grading won't let you type a promotion from told to lived. It's not forbidden by a policy. It's ill-typed.

  The concept map: "Why provenance cannot be laundered. No operator, no sequence of operators, no interleaving can increase lived mass without a Fit event. You can't promote
  told to lived because the algebra's grading won't let you type it. You can't create lived from nothing because only Fit mints it. These aren't policies. They're theorems."

  The paper (gerundelle.tex, line ~106): the six fields whose mathematics describe the architecture include "information security" as one of them. The provenance layer's
  guarantees are stated in the language of information-flow security: non-interference between the imagination register and the funded web, and conservation of provenance
  weight across all operations.

  The prior-art report confirms this is novel in cognitive architecture (Verified cognitive architecture prior art.md): "I found no cognitive architecture that uses semiring
  provenance or non-interference as its backbone. Searches for that combination returned only computer-security architectures (e.g. the machine-checked SAFE information-flow
  architecture)."

  Where it's exercised in the running system:

  - The chess ladder's rung 1b: imagination (Params.imagine). The mind imagines ahead using Stockfish's word — but nothing imagined is written in the register. Imagined
    outcomes only score plans (the planner's model reads them at the mind's credulity). The firewall holds: nine of ten minds that imagined with Stockfish never learned the
    defender's condition, because imagination steered them away from the lived contrast they needed. Competence came from the teller's word; the register stayed empty.
  - The fiction ladder's rung 2: told knowledge from Ben. His answer enters Mara's register as a told receipt, funded at half. When her own tries confirm it, the told receipt
    stays told — but a new lived receipt is added alongside it. The confirmation is lived. The original telling stays at its original weight. No laundering.
  - The civilization layer (SOV_CIVILIZATIONS.md): "Told knowledge degrades with distance. The home world's agricultural conditions are told to the colony. But the colony's
    soil is different. The told receipts contradict on first planting. The colony re-funds locally." Across generations, told knowledge that is never tested stays at half
    weight. What is tested becomes lived. What is taught forward without testing halves again.

  How it differs from information security specifically:

  - In security, non-interference protects secrets (the high level) from leaking to observers (the low level). Here, the protection runs the other way: the funded web
    (understanding) is protected from contamination by imagination (unfunded ideas). The "secret" is what the mind has actually experienced; the "public" is what it imagined.
    The guarantee is that imagination can't infiltrate understanding.
  - In security, the enforcement is access control or type systems in the implementation. Here, it's the algebra itself: the graded writer monad's grading makes the promotion
    a type error. The Lean proofs check it for every possible sequence of operations.
  - The security property has a direct cognitive consequence: a mind that only imagines, reasons, and reflects without acting can never increase its understanding. Only acting
    in the world — a Fit event — mints lived receipts. This is a structural answer to "why can't you think your way to understanding?"
