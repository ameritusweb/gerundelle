> Where do Gibson's affordances fit into the system?

● Here's where it lands in the running system.

  Gibson's ecological psychology (1979): an affordance is what the environment offers an organism for action — a chair affords sitting, a handle affords grasping. Affordances
  are perceived directly from the environment's structure, not inferred from properties like shape or material. They are relations between the environment and the organism,
  not properties of either alone.

  The world model's open force slots ARE affordances. In force_world.py, every object is a balance of external forces against internal reserves (grip, capacity, seal). When a
  force crosses a reserve the gerund flips: charged becomes empty, resting becomes falling. Where a configuration invites a force that has not been applied — a button to be
  pressed, a lever with no one at its long arm — the world has an open force slot. The paper says it explicitly (gerundelle.tex, line ~185): "an open force slot computes what
  Gibson described as an affordance."

  The invitations the world offers (world.invitations in chessworld.py, force_world.py) are the affordances — they're in the world, not in the mind. In the chess world, the
  legal moves are the invitations. In the force world, every object that accepts a push is an invitation. The what_ifs.txt entry on forces-as-world-model is this idea: "use
  forces, net forces, and potential forces as a proxy for affordances, where positions and distances are inferred from the force structure."

  The concept map (center-left column): "Gerund ASTs: every node is a gerund with typed force slots, not a noun with properties. A stone arch and a steel beam share one
  skeleton. Material doesn't appear in the representation; only what things are doing under forces." That material invariance is what makes the affordance abstract: a latch
  and a bolt both "fasten," and the mind that learned one transfers to the other by shape.

  How it differs from Gibson specifically:

  - Gibson's affordances are organism-relative (a chair affords sitting for a human, not for a fish). Here the force slots are objective — they're in the world's physics, not
    relativized to the agent's body. What's agent-relative is the perception of affordances: the Umwelt factors through the agent's invariances, so different agents perceive
    different subsets of the world's force slots. The affordance is objective; the perception of it is not.
  - Gibson resisted the idea of mental representation — affordances are perceived directly. Here, the world's force slots are perceived and then represented as gerund ASTs in
    the mind. The mind has a representation. But the representation is of doings (gerunds), not of things (nouns with properties), which is closer to Gibson's spirit than a
    traditional object model.
  - Gibson didn't give a mechanism for how affordances compose. Here, gerund ASTs nest: narrating contains explaining contains describing. Composition combines structures
    (cognitivecode rung 7: a header from one seed with a growth from another produced programs search never found). The compositionality is what makes the representation
    generative.
