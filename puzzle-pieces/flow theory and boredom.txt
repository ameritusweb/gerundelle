> Where does Csikszentmihalyi's flow theory fit into the system, and what is boredom?

● Here's where it lands in the running system.

  Flow theory (Csikszentmihalyi, 1990): flow is the state of optimal experience — complete absorption in what you're doing. It arises when the challenge of the task matches
  your skill. Too little challenge for your skill: boredom. Too much challenge for your skill: anxiety. The "flow channel" is the diagonal where the two are balanced.

  The system has measurable readouts for all three states.

  Boredom is a measured quantity: Mind.readouts (molecules.py, line ~706) computes it as the mean surprise of the winners at level 0. When the winners are all familiar —
  well-explored pairs with concentrated Dirichlets and low surprise — boredom is low (close to 0). Five consecutive ticks below 0.05 triggers the meta layer's "bored"
  emission. The "bored" meta molecule's operation (Meta.operate, line ~1359): novelty is doubled for five ticks (mind.boost_until = mind.t + 5). The mind notices "nothing's
  happening" and responds by raising the pull of untried pairings. This is the mind's own structural response to boredom — not a parameter the designer tweaks, but a
  meta-cognitive detection and intervention.

  Anxiety is a phase (Phases.graph, line ~2552): "a concept that matters, its outcomes mostly bad, understood below 0.4." A concept the mind keeps encountering, that keeps
  going wrong, and that is poorly understood — the mind doesn't know enough to predict what will happen, and what does happen is mostly bad. Anxiety forms as a phase molecule
  anchored on the concept, competing in the phase track (Params.phase_slots: 2 → 1 → 1). It closes when understanding reaches 0.5 — when the mind learns enough to stop
  being surprised by the bad outcomes.

  Flow is what happens in between, and it has two readouts:

  1. The novelty_share (Mind.readouts): for each winner, what fraction of its binding strength comes from novelty vs experience and fit. In flow, the share is moderate — some
     exploration (the task is challenging enough to have untried pairings), some exploitation (the skill is high enough that many pairings are understood). High novelty share:
     the mind is overwhelmed by the unknown (challenge >> skill). Low novelty share: everything is familiar (skill >> challenge).

  2. The flat_for counter (line ~629): consecutive ticks a molecule's surprise level stayed flat (within Params.flat = 0.05). A molecule that has won the top for three ticks
     with flat surprise settles into storage (Mind.settled, Params.dwell = 3). In flow, surprises happen often enough that thoughts don't settle prematurely, and
     understanding grows through each surprise. The settled closure is the end of flow for that thought: nothing left to learn. The next thought enters.

  The fiction ladder's rung 4 showed the absence of flow as a diagnostic: "Variety is lowest here (0.31-0.36): the stretch where Mara tries the lid and the flag between
  contests is told try by try." The mind was in the boredom zone — the same pushes on the same objects, nothing surprising, but the want was unmet so it couldn't stop. The
  printer's "explore" summary ("Again and again she tried the lid and the flag, but nothing came of it") was the telling solution, but the mind's experience was genuinely
  repetitive. The contested region kept reopening the want, but nothing new happened on the way.

  The chess ladder showed the anxiety end. Rung 1c (the back rank puzzle): five seeds converge and find the mate; two never do; seed 5 loops. Seed 5 learned a coincidental
  condition ("Re8 mates when the f7 pawn is attacked"), set the sub-want, and met it with Rd7 326 times — the same move, every episode, no progress, but the sub-want kept
  being served. This is not boredom (the mind is acting, spending resources) and not flow (nothing is learned). It's a stuck state that the meta layer detects: "stuck" emits
  after 3 blocked ticks, and "looping" when a molecule holds the top 3 of the last 5 ticks.

  The calm phase (Phases.graph, line ~2577) is the positive residue of flow's end: "nothing blocked, surprise flat, five ticks running" — the mind has finished its work,
  nothing is pulling for attention, and the pool is quiet. Not bored (boredom triggers a boost); calm. The mind reached understanding and has nothing left to learn right now.

  How it differs from flow theory specifically:

  - Csikszentmihalyi's flow is described as a subjective experience: loss of self-consciousness, time distortion, intrinsic motivation. Here, there is no subjective
    experience — only measurable readouts (boredom, novelty_share, flat_for, anxiety phase) and structural responses (the boredom boost, the anxiety closure at
    understanding 0.5, the calm phase). What flows is the competition itself: a balanced tournament where enough is known to act effectively and enough is unknown to keep
    learning.
  - Flow theory places the flow channel on a 2D space of challenge × skill. Here, the relevant dimensions are different: surprise (how unexpected the outcomes are) and
    understanding (how funded the relation slots are). Boredom is low surprise, high understanding. Anxiety is high surprise, low understanding. Flow is moderate surprise,
    growing understanding. But the dimensions are not independent — understanding reduces future surprise naturally, so the mind moves along the learning curve, not across a
    static grid.
  - Flow theory doesn't specify a mechanism for how the mind returns from boredom or anxiety. Here, the boredom boost (novelty doubled for 5 ticks) is a specific structural
    intervention by the meta layer. Anxiety closes when understanding passes its threshold (0.5). The transitions are mechanisms, not just descriptions.
  - The distinction between boredom (nothing is surprising) and being stuck (something is pulling but nothing works) is structural. Boredom triggers exploration (the novelty
    boost). Being stuck triggers parking (removing the molecule from competition and keying it so it returns when circumstances change). Csikszentmihalyi's model doesn't
    distinguish these — both would register as "challenge too low" or "challenge too high."
